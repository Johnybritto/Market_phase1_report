## Ion Exchange — Roha Turnaround Thesis and Mandatory Revalidation Dates

### Current thesis
Ion Exchange is not currently preferred over WABAG on execution quality, but it has meaningful upside if the Roha resin facility successfully ramps. Roha is already commissioned; the investment case now depends on utilisation, fixed-cost absorption, chemicals-margin recovery, cash-flow improvement and debt stabilisation.

### What would make Ion more attractive
- Roha utilisation reaches roughly 25%+ and continues toward 40%.
- Chemicals segment margin improves sequentially.
- Consolidated EBITDA margin recovers from roughly 7.2% FY26 toward 8.5–9%+, eventually 9–10%+.
- PAT returns to sustainable double-digit growth.
- Operating cash flow becomes positive/improves.
- Gross debt stabilises and begins falling.
- Legacy low-margin engineering projects materially wind down.
- Export resin demand/customer repeat orders become visible.
- Semiconductor/high-tech UPW orders are upside, not a prerequisite for the thesis.

### Thesis failure / warning
Do not buy the turnaround merely because utilisation rises. If Roha ramps but chemicals margin, consolidated EBITDA margin and operating cash flow do not improve, the investment thesis is not validated.

### Mandatory revalidation windows
These are rough result windows and must be rechecked against the company's actual exchange-announced board/result date when available.

1. **Q2 FY27:** approximately **28 October–6 November 2026**
   - First serious checkpoint for Roha utilisation, chemicals margin, EBITDA recovery, legacy engineering drag, cash flow and debt.
2. **Q3 FY27:** approximately **25 January–2 February 2027**
   - Decisive checkpoint: confirm whether Q2 improvement is becoming a trend rather than one-quarter timing.
3. **Q4 / FY27:** approximately **25–31 May 2027**
   - Full-year validation if the thesis is still unresolved.

### Early decision rule
If by Q3 FY27 the combination is:
- Roha utilisation rising,
- chemicals margin rising,
- operating cash flow positive/improving,
then immediately re-run a full Phase 3 and compare Ion Exchange against the entire Tier A water universe for incremental capital.

If utilisation rises but margins/cash flow do not improve, do not reward the Roha narrative.
