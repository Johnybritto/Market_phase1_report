**Purpose:** Permanent listed-equity research universe and **single source of truth for water-theme rankings, thesis status and water-related research activity**. This is a research watchlist, not a buy list. All capital decisions remain governed by `MASTER_INVESTMENT_RULES.md` and the full Phase 3 framework.

## Rule

The water check must not be limited to VA Tech WABAG and Ion Exchange. Every weekly holdings review, Phase 1 report and other mandatory water-theme check must scan the Tier A direct/core universe below and may surface any company that offers better current risk/reward than WABAG or Ion Exchange.

Do not give WABAG or Ion Exchange automatic preference. Every company must compete for incremental capital based on business quality, growth runway, earnings quality, cash conversion, balance sheet, valuation, portfolio fit and entry price.

### Mandatory persistence rule

Whenever a weekly holdings review, Phase 1/Phase 3 analysis, magazine analysis, earnings review, order/news review or other research produces a **material water-theme finding**, update this file in the same workflow.

This file must contain the latest:
- Tier A/B/C membership and additions/removals.
- Water-company ranking and current action/status.
- Best business quality.
- Best current risk/reward / entry candidate.
- Most improved thesis.
- Biggest deterioration / avoid-fresh-buy candidate.
- Material changes in orders, recurring/O&M/consumables revenue, earnings, margins, cash flow, receivables, debt, execution, policy/capex, valuation and technical entry risk.
- Verified second-order water correlations from semiconductors, data centres, power, chemicals, electronics, manufacturing, infrastructure and urbanisation.
- Phase 3 promotion/demotion decisions.

A material water finding must **not live only inside an individual weekly report**. Update `WATER_THEME_UNIVERSE.md` so subsequent reports inherit the latest research state. Do not overwrite older thesis context silently: update the current ranking/status and append the dated activity log below.

## Tier A — Direct / Core Water Universe — Mandatory Weekly Scan

- Direct exposure: municipal/industrial water treatment, desalination, wastewater reuse, high-purity water, EPC and O&M.
- Key checks: order intake/quality, O&M share, international mix, cash flow, receivables, net cash/debt, margins, desalination/reuse/high-purity opportunities, valuation and technical entry.

### Ion Exchange (India) (`IONEXCHANG`)
- Direct exposure: industrial/municipal water treatment, resins, membranes, chemicals, ZLD/reuse and high-purity water.
- Key checks: Roha utilisation, chemicals margins, legacy engineering drag, operating cash flow, debt, export resin demand and UPW/high-tech opportunities.

### EMS Limited (`EMSLIMITED`)
- Direct exposure: sewage/wastewater treatment, water-supply schemes, STPs/WTPs/CETPs, pumping/pipelines and O&M.
- Key checks: order book, funded-project quality, O&M share, working capital/receivables, geographic concentration, margins, L1-to-order conversion and cash conversion.

### Enviro Infra Engineers (`EIEL`)
- Direct exposure: WWTP/STP/WSSP, water-supply projects and O&M; increasingly diversified into renewables/BESS.
- Key checks: water versus renewables mix, water order book, O&M annuity, revenue-to-PAT conversion, margins, working capital, debt, execution and valuation.

### Concord Enviro Systems (`CEWATER`)
- Direct exposure: industrial wastewater, ZLD, reuse, membranes, O&M/consumables and water-treatment technology.
- Key checks: systems/plants versus recurring O&M/consumables, international mix, proprietary membrane/IP position, order pipeline, cash conversion, customer concentration, profitability and valuation/liquidity.

### Denta Water & Infra Solutions (`DENTA`)
- Direct exposure: water management, groundwater recharge, drinking-water and wastewater infrastructure, with irrigation exposure.
- Key checks: water share of order book, execution, inventory/working-capital growth, receivables, margins, cash flow, order quality and customer/state concentration.

### SPML Infra (`SPMLINFRA`)
- Direct exposure: water-supply, treatment, pumping/distribution, irrigation and long-duration O&M; also diversifying into BESS.
- Key checks: turnaround quality, debt, cash flow, JV economics, order execution, O&M share, receivables, margins, funded-order quality and dilution/governance risk.

### Welspun Enterprises (`WELENT`)
- Direct exposure: large municipal water/wastewater EPC projects plus long-duration O&M, alongside roads/infrastructure.
- Key checks: Dharavi WWTP, Bhandup/Panjarpur WTP execution, O&M annuity, water-business share, project cash flows, debt, order visibility and valuation versus non-water businesses.

### Jash Engineering (`JASH`)
- Direct/technology-equipment exposure: water-control gates, screens, valves, process equipment and other water/wastewater infrastructure equipment with international exposure.
- Key checks: order book, export share, margins, manufacturing capacity, product moat, aftermarket/spares, cash conversion, customer/geographic concentration and valuation.

### WPIL (`WPIL`)
- Direct equipment/system exposure: pumps and integrated pumping systems used in water supply, irrigation, sewage, industrial and infrastructure projects; global operations.
- Key checks: order book, domestic water/irrigation share, international mix, margin quality, acquisition integration, working capital, debt and valuation.

### Vishnu Prakash R Punglia (`VPRPL`)
- Direct EPC exposure: water-supply, sewerage, WTP/pipelines and related infrastructure, with other civil segments.
- Key checks: water share of revenue/order book, new-order quality, execution, receivables, working capital, margins, customer/state concentration and balance sheet.
